Original sci-fi suspense micro-films

Anomaly Archive Studio is an independent, one-person studio that writes and produces original short films — "anomaly archive" files, surveillance-style rule stories and dark sci-fi — for its own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Anomaly Archive Studio 是個人獨立經營的微電影工作室，製作原創的規則怪談、異常收容檔案與暗黑科幻短片， 只發布於工作室自有的 YouTube 頻道與 Instagram 帳號。

What this website is for

This site documents the studio and hosts its Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. The studio uses a private, self-hosted toolchain on the owner's own computer to:

  • publish finished videos as Shorts and Reels to the studio's own YouTube channel and Instagram professional account, through the publishing service Upload-Post;
  • read performance data (views, audience retention, average watch time, shares, saves) and comments on the studio's own posts, to improve future episodes.

本網站用於說明工作室並提供隱私權政策與服務條款。工作室在負責人自己的電腦上執行私人自動化工具， 透過發布服務 Upload-Post 把影片發到「自己的」頻道與帳號，並讀取自己作品的成效數據與留言，以改善後續作品。

Not a public app

The toolchain is not offered to other users. It never signs in to, reads from, or posts to anyone else's account. It never sells data, and the only thing it shares is the studio's own videos and captions, sent to its publishing service.

此工具不對外提供、沒有其他使用者，不會登入或存取任何他人的帳號；絕不販售資料， 唯一對外傳送的只有工作室自己的影片與文案（交給發布服務 Upload-Post）。

Content standards

  • All stories, characters and settings are original works.
  • No graphic violence or gore — suspense is built through atmosphere, not depiction.
  • Visuals are made with AI generation tools and are disclosed as AI-generated or synthetic content on each platform.

所有故事皆為原創；不含血腥暴力畫面；畫面使用 AI 生成工具製作，並依各平台規範標示為 AI 生成／合成內容。
